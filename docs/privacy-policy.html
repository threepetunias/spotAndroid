What Spot collects

Nothing. Spot does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The exercises, gym names, weights, sets, reps, cues, notes and tags you enter are stored only on your phone. Tags and notes can describe health issues if you choose to write them that way. They are never sent anywhere by Spot.

Backups

Spot can save a backup file when you ask it to. You choose where the file goes. Spot does not upload it. Your phone's own backup service (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include Spot's data if you have that turned on in your phone's settings. That is controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by Spot.

Links to other apps

If you turn on the Tend link, Spot opens the Tend app on your phone. Spot does not send data to Tend.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting the Spot app removes its data from your phone. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

Children

Spot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not a medical device

Spot is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Talk to a doctor or other healthcare professional about pain, injury or any health concern.
